Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use ToolGuideHub (the “site”).

Your input stays in your browser

Tools on this site run in your browser. Text, files and numbers you enter are processed on your device and are not sent to us by the tools. A few tools load open-source helper libraries from public CDNs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net or unpkg.com); when that happens your browser contacts that CDN to download the library, and the CDN may see your IP address.

Data stored on your device

The site uses your browser’s local storage to remember your favourite tools, recently viewed tools and your advertising choice. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under its own privacy statement. We do not run our own analytics.

Advertising

The site is prepared to show Google AdSense ads in the future but shows none while no AdSense account is configured. If ads are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them if you allow it. You will be asked for your choice before ads load, and you can learn more or opt out at Google’s advertising policy page and Ads Settings.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is usually nothing to access or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us through the Contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
