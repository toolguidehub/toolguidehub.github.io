Pricing

Simple: there is nothing to buy.

Every tool is free

All tools in the ToolGuideHub directory are free to use. There are no accounts, no subscriptions and no paywalled features. Tools run in your browser, so your files and text are not uploaded to a server by the tool itself.

How the site is funded

The site may show advertising in the future. If it does, ads are clearly labelled and kept away from the tool controls. Ads never change which tools are listed or how they rank.

Paid or premium plans

None exist today. If that changes, this page will be updated before anything is charged.
